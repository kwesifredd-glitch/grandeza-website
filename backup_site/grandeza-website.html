WHO WE ARE

Building to Empower & Foster Development

Grandeza Construction Limited incorporated in Ghana as a limited liability company in 2023 undertakes Building and Civil Engineering works.

Our main aim is not only to build but to inspire and empower people to foster national development. Our Engineers and Project Management team are of sound professional standing and have the capabilities to manage projects from inception, design, construction and commissioning.
