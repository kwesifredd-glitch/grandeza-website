OUR STORY

Who We Are

Grandeza Construction Limited incorporated in Ghana as a limited liability company in 2023 undertakes Building and Civil Engineering works.

Our main aim is not only to build but to inspire and empower people to foster national development. Our Engineers and Project Management team are of sound professional standing and have the capabilities to manage projects from inception, design, construction and commissioning.

Our Mission

To deliver high-quality residential, commercial, industrial, and community construction projects, driven by a team of skilled and motivated craftsmen.

Our Vision

To become Ghana’s leading force in construction by building sustainable spaces that empower people and drive economic growth and development.

SAFETY FIRST

Grandeza Health & Safety Policy

Grandeza Safe Systems of Work: The Company policy guidelines on health and safety i.e. Grandeza Safe Systems of Work states that all employees including management conduct their activities in such a way as to take foremost account of the health and safety of the individual and other persons.

A safe system of work is a formal procedure which results from systematic examination of a task in order to identify all the hazards. It defines safe methods to ensure that hazards are eliminated or risks minimised.

Grandeza Construction Ltd is committed to building efficiently, on schedule, and in full compliance with contractual, statutory, and safety standards.
